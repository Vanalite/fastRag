# Q3 Board Summary (Confidential — Finance)

Prepared by the CFO office for the Northwind Labs board meeting. Distribution is restricted to the finance group.

## Headline numbers

Q3 revenue was 18.4 million USD, up 22 percent year over year and 6 percent above plan. Annual recurring revenue reached 71 million USD at quarter end. Gross margin improved to 78 percent from 74 percent a year ago, mainly because of lower hosting costs after the storage migration.

Operating expenses were 15.9 million USD. The operating loss narrowed to 1.6 million USD, and the company expects to be cash-flow positive in Q2 of next year.

## Customers

Net revenue retention was 118 percent. The company signed 14 new enterprise customers, including two in the public sector. Gross churn was 4 percent, concentrated among small business accounts on the legacy pricing plan.

## Cash and runway

Cash and equivalents stood at 42 million USD at the end of September, which gives roughly 30 months of runway at the current burn rate. No new financing is planned in the next 12 months.

## Risks

- A large renewal with a logistics customer, worth 2.1 million USD per year, is at risk because of a competing bid.
- Hiring for senior engineering roles is running eight weeks behind plan.

## Board asks

The board is asked to approve the FY budget for next year and a 5 million USD increase to the hiring plan for the platform team.
