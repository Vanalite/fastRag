# Travel and Expense Policy

Owner: Finance Operations. Applies to all Northwind Labs employees and contractors who travel on company business.

## Booking travel

All flights, hotels and rental cars must be booked through the Northwind travel portal, TripDesk. Bookings made outside TripDesk are reimbursed only when the portal had no availability and the traveller attaches a screenshot showing that. Book flights at least 14 days before departure whenever possible.

Economy class is the default for every flight. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than six hours. Business class requires written approval from a vice president before booking.

## Hotels and meals

The nightly hotel cap is 250 USD in most cities and 350 USD in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich and Tokyo). The daily meal allowance is 75 USD per person, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client dinner.

## Ground transport

Use public transit or ride-share where practical. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at the published federal rate. Parking at the airport is reimbursed for up to five days per trip.

## Submitting expenses

Submit every expense report in Ledgerly within 30 days of the trip ending. Each line item over 25 USD needs an itemized receipt; a credit card statement alone is not a receipt. Reports submitted more than 90 days late are rejected and not reimbursed.

Your direct manager approves reports up to 5,000 USD. Reports above 5,000 USD also need approval from the Finance Operations team. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## Corporate card

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a Northwind corporate card. Personal purchases on the corporate card are not allowed and must be repaid within 10 days.
