# Engineering Compensation Bands (Confidential — HR)

Owner: People Team, Total Rewards. Access is restricted to the HR group. Do not share outside HR.

## Base salary bands (USD, US locations)

| Level | Title | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E3 | Software Engineer | 120,000 | 140,000 | 160,000 |
| E4 | Senior Software Engineer | 155,000 | 180,000 | 205,000 |
| E5 | Staff Engineer | 195,000 | 225,000 | 255,000 |
| E6 | Principal Engineer | 240,000 | 275,000 | 310,000 |

Bands for locations outside the US are set with a geographic multiplier, published by Total Rewards each January.

## Equity

New hires at E5 and above receive an initial equity grant that vests over four years with a one-year cliff. Annual refresh grants are decided during the performance cycle.

## Rules for offers

Offers above the band midpoint need approval from the hiring manager's director. Offers above the band maximum are not allowed; level the candidate higher instead or decline. Sign-on bonuses are capped at 30,000 USD.

## Annual review

Compensation is reviewed once a year in March. Merit increases are funded from a pool of 4 percent of payroll. Employees below the band minimum are brought up to it as part of the review.
