# Paid Time Off Policy

Owner: People Team. Applies to all full-time Northwind Labs employees.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, earned at 1.67 days per month. After five years of service the accrual rises to 25 days per year. Part-time employees accrue vacation pro rata to their scheduled hours.

Unused vacation carries over into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any balance above the carryover cap is forfeited on 31 March of the following year.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from vacation. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, which do not carry over. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than three consecutive working days.

## Company holidays

Northwind observes 11 public holidays each year, published on the intranet every December. The office is also closed between 24 December and 1 January; those days do not count against your vacation balance.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 12 weeks. Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first year after a birth or adoption.

## Requesting time off

Request vacation in Workday at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Your manager approves or declines the request within five working days. During the end-of-quarter code freeze, engineers should avoid scheduling vacation unless they have arranged cover with their team lead.

## Leaving Northwind

When you leave the company, unused accrued vacation is paid out in your final paycheck where local law requires it.
